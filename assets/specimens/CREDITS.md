# Specimen plate credits

The wallpaper's fish and corals are cut from these plates by `scripts/generate-specimens.tsx`. All are in the public domain (the artists died more than 100 years ago, and the scans are faithful reproductions). Copies here are downscaled to 2000px tall; the originals are on Wikimedia Commons.
