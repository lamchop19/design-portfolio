I spent Fall 2025 studying abroad at NYU Sydney as an Intercultural Learning Fellow. The
role was to develop an educational project about Australian life and culture for future
students, alongside hosting programming that encouraged people to actually explore the
city and build community in it.

## My approach

American media tends to flatten Australian culture into a handful of stereotypes. I
wanted to push against that with something concrete, so I built a set of postcards from
photography and graphic design across several Sydney neighborhoods — the multicultural
city that popular portrayals leave out.

I shot and edited in Photoshop, and built the graphic elements in Illustrator and Figma.
Each postcard carries a note on why that community matters, so the design does not have
to carry the context alone.

## Takeaways

This was the clearest demonstration I have had of visual design as a way of constructing
a narrative rather than decorating one. As a visitor on Aboriginal land, in a country
shaped by immigration, I was aware that I was making choices about how Australia gets
portrayed — and I hope the postcards leave exchange students with a fuller sense of its
cultural range than the one they arrive with.
