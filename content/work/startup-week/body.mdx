Startup Week is a sub-brand of tech@nyu focused on bringing innovative, emerging startups
to campus. It showcases student perspectives on design, engineering and growth, and its
centerpiece event — the Startup Week Buildathon — drew over 200 participants competing
for $26k+ in prizes.

## My approach

Startup Week needed to read as its own thing while still belonging to tech@nyu. I built
a social media brand kit — assets plus guidelines — that would set it apart: color,
typography, and a graphic language it could be recognized by.

Working with one other designer, I kept everything in a single Figma file. That mattered
more than it sounds: the team had uneven software proficiency, and a shared file with
clear guidelines meant output stayed consistent regardless of who was producing it.

The campaign included four Instagram reels that generated over 20,000 views, with
longer-form video following.

## Takeaways

The strength of the Startup Week brand proved to be extremely resonant with NYU students
and even those beyond our community — we drew participants from Columbia, UPenn and
Cornell. Branding it as a destination rather than a club event is what made the following
year noticeably larger.
