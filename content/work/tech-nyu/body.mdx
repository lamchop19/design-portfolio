tech@nyu is NYU's largest tech community. Over 2025 I led its marketing, first as
Marketing Lead and then as Vice President, rebuilding how the organization presented
itself across brand and social.

## My approach

tech@nyu had a strong social presence on paper — a large following built over years —
but engagement told a different story. Reach was concentrated in people who already
knew us, and posts read as announcements rather than invitations.

I ran a competitive analysis against peer organizations and a SWOT exercise on our own
channels. The clearest gap was LinkedIn: every comparable club was optimizing for
Instagram, which left an opening to differentiate by showcasing the community itself —
the people, the projects, the work — rather than just event logistics.

From there I set a posting cadence the team could actually sustain, produced the
graphics, and edited video for the channels where it mattered most.

## Takeaways

Brand positioning is a decision about who you are talking to, and it is easy to default
to the people already in the room. The posts that worked were the ones written for
someone who had never been to an event — community-first and explicitly inclusive,
rather than assuming shared context.
