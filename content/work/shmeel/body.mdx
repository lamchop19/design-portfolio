Shmeel NYC (now Jeu de Paume) was a New York streetwear brand with 40,000+ Instagram
followers. I designed a product lookbook for retail outreach, translating the brand's
visual narrative for online and brick-and-mortar retailers.

## My approach

I worked directly with Shmeel's founder to understand the brand from the inside. Their
Summer 2024 collection centered on beauty and harmony in sport, accented by the brand's
90s New York influences.

Because this was an internal document for retailers rather than a consumer piece, I had
real freedom in curating images — I chose the ones that best carried the brand's story.
I structured the lookbook around the moods of the clothing itself, using imagery to pace
the catalog and separate the craftsman-like tennis pieces from the New York–centered ones.

Shmeel also had no established typographic identity. To hold the whole thing together I
set it in an editorial serif, which matched the elevated-streetwear register the
collection was reaching for.

## Takeaways

Working in an accelerated two-week timeframe, I produced a cohesive, functional lookbook
Shmeel could take straight to retailers. It clarified how differently an internal
document behaves from a consumer-facing one, and it was the project where design started
to look like a business tool to me — a way for a brand to tell its story to whichever
stakeholder is in the room.
